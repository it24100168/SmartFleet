## Page 6: minimum Agentic AI workflow

The specification explicitly permits a custom orchestration approach; it does not explicitly require an LLM. SmartFleet's [ADR](../adr/003-orchestration-and-state.md) describes a deterministic, typed, rule-based implementation. Whether the assessor regards its fixed-template planning as sufficiently agentic remains a material interpretation risk; seek written lecturer clarification rather than claiming an LLM exists.
